# Match-3 battle modes and exit behavior

Updated 2026-09-21 from offline inspection of packaged PNC **5.0.203 / versionCode 233**. Evidence root: `.local-data/apk-exploration/gameplay-lua/` in the main repository. No current live battle, Auto unlock or Daily-credit result was observed in this review. Downloaded updates may change the packaged behavior.

## Requested behavior

**User-confirmed, high confidence, 2026-09-21:** Campaign, Arena and Lost Land must each support our solver, early exit for fast Daily progress, and in-game Auto when unlocked. Time Rift was explicitly deferred. These are requirements, not evidence that every control or early-exit credit rule is qualified. The [match-3 epic](../../../plans/themed/gameplay/PNC_MATCH3_COMPONENT_PLAN.md) owns all delivery: M1 includes lifecycle and pure rules/combat selection, M2 implements Auto/Exit, M3 supplies feature/Daily bindings, and M4 integrates the solver and qualifies operation. The [solver design reference](../../../plans/themed/gameplay/PNC_MATCH3_BATTLE_SOLVER_PLAN.md) retains algorithm boundaries and rule evidence without separate milestones. V13/V14 and V30 require selectable-mode API handoff tests only at the battle boundary; their completion does not imply implemented or qualified battle modes.

The original source-derived board findings remain: adjacent ordinary swaps must form the supported matches; specials are clicked. Straight five or a qualifying five-cell T/L creates color-clear, straight four creates a 3 × 3 clear, and a 2 × 2 square creates a five-cell cross clear, with color > nine-grid > cross creation precedence. Sources: `puzzleboard.lua` `CheckHasBoom` / `CheckColorBoom` / `CheckNineGridBoom` / `CheckCrossBoom` / `CheckHasSpecialBoom`, and `puzzlecell.lua` drag/click handlers, under `uis/puzzlebattle/battles/battleunits/puzzles/`. These are **artifact-observed** for the inspected rule path; exact server spawn placement, chaining, refills and per-context application remain unqualified. The existing plan also records the user's confirmation of the three special effects. The retained cross-effect contract clears the activated cell and its four immediate orthogonal neighbours, clipped at board edges; it does not clear the full row and column.

- **Repository-proven, high:** `CampaignExecutionMode` in `app/pnc/domain/daily_maintenance.py` selects `fixed_stage` or `progress_then_farm`. The new battle-mode choice is separate from this target-selection policy.
- **Repository-proven, high:** `daily_quest_catalog.py` groups Campaign challenge/AP/chapter-clear labels under one quest ID. `HERO_ARENA` labels and the Daily plan refer to Hero Showdown; that is not proof they map to the requested match-3 Arena surface. Preserve the exact observed objective and qualify the route before associating a battle with it.
- **Repository-proven, high:** the [Daily plan](../../../plans/themed/account-runtime/PNC_DAILY_CASTLE_MAINTENANCE_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) records Lost Land static route captures and requires Daily-row advancement after Challenge. It does not establish early-exit credit. Lost Land, Land of Trial and V33 Lost City Headquarters are separate scopes.
- **Unknown:** exact current Lost Land source-family mapping, its qualified active-battle/exit/Auto controls and outcome rules; current Arena subtype; context-specific retreat cost/refund/credit; current Auto unlock state. Names alone do not settle these questions.

The next useful evidence is a saved or separately authorized context-specific before/after sequence: exact entry and target, active battle and controls, any confirmation, actual result/return, and the exact Daily row before/after when testing fast exit. An Auto sequence also records unlocked/on-state and absence of automatic next-battle entry. Reuse adequate shared-control evidence; do not spend to populate every context/mode combination merely for bookkeeping. The retained Campaign one-attempt/20-AP allocation is not a budget for the additional contexts or modes.
