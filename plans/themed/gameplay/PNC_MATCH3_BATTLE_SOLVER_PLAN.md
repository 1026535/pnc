# Campaign match-3 battle solver plan

**Status:** implementation plan, 2026-09-21. **Base:** `76a6957a91120fdfaf58b6ca1f411bfb61b520d8` (`main`). This plan is the canonical owner for match-board rules, battle-state interpretation, move selection, and the Campaign battle loop. The existing Campaign navigation and formation plans retain ownership of their routes. The dropped `plans/dropped/PNC_CAMPAIGN_ARENA_MATCH_SOLVER_SUBPLAN.md` is historical context, not a second specification.

## Outcome and decisions

Build one repository-native, typed solver and a full Campaign battle loop. Repeatedly observe the settled board and combat state, choose one legal swap or special-tile click, execute it through the existing GUI input owner, and reobserve until independently recognized victory or defeat. Move choice estimates multi-turn survival and win chances from board-driven attacks, targeting, color interactions, health, and turn pressure; it does not promise a win or exact server outcomes. Use every visibly ready hero skill, including ordinary target selection where the UI requires it, then reobserve. Hero-effect prediction and Arena entry/execution are separate follow-up work, not parallel solver implementations.

The one authorized live acceptance target is the currently selected unlocked Campaign stage on the active configured `testing` instance/castle. The ceiling is **one battle attempt and 20 AP total**. If the stage, castle, cost, or resulting action is uncertain, or the cost exceeds 20 AP, stop before battle entry. No castle switch, AP purchase/refill, formation edit, battle replay, or reward claim is authorized by this plan.

Battle entry needs a typed, durable authorization for that castle, observed stage, one attempt, and at most 20 AP. The existing Daily/feature acknowledgement and intent types count operations and diamonds, not AP; do not encode AP as diamonds or treat an in-memory cost check as the budget. Reuse the canonical authorization/journal machinery by extracting its shared contract where needed, with Campaign-owned AP fields and a pre-tap reservation of the observed cost and attempt. A dispatched battle-start input consumes the sole attempt even if the server rejects it; release reserved AP only on an authoritative no-spend result. An ambiguous outcome keeps the AP reservation consumed until reconciled and never grants an automatic replay.

## Implementation

1. **Battle observations and evidence.** Add independently identifiable board-active, resolving/turn-blocked, victory, and defeat states through the existing screen-classification and observation pipeline; publish one frame-bound typed board, measured cell centers/bounds, elemental colors, special function, enemy positions/HP/turn cues, own HP, and hero readiness only when actually visible. Keep geometry/provenance outside the pure solver. Make both existing observation publishers agree on the same facts; an unknown or occluded decision-critical cell blocks input rather than becoming a guessed color. Use portable authored fixtures for rules and any existing captures for navigation; the first real board capture is an evidence gate for tile classification and combat calibration. Document its date/build and distinguish observed facts from source-derived inference.
2. **One canonical rule engine.** Introduce a pure immutable board/action/evaluation contract, with one move generator that checks each adjacent pair once and emits only legal swaps plus clicks on observed special tiles. Resolve immediate matches and the creation patterns above, including overlapping patterns and board-edge clipping; a color-clearing tile affects its own observed color, whereas nine-grid and cross effects are spatial and color-independent. Keep creation, activation, and scoring separate concerns, but do not duplicate pattern detection or create Campaign/Arena forks. Verify tile placement and any chained-special behavior from reported frames before modeling it; unsupported observed combinations fail closed. Do not simulate an exact random refill or claim an unverified cascade.
3. **Combat model and selector.** Trace the packaged client’s board-to-army targeting, color relationship, enemy retaliation/turn cadence, and HP updates; use actual UI or report traces to calibrate server-controlled damage. Represent observed health/target/turn state separately from inferred damage and refill distributions. Evaluate bounded multi-turn outcomes/estimated win and survival chances for each legal action using documented assumptions for unknown refills, and rebase estimates on the next observed frame. Rank by estimated win chance, then survival, then expected damage/resource gain, then stable board coordinates; retain an explanation and uncertainty with the chosen action. Do not manufacture exact damage formulas or precise probabilities when evidence is insufficient: mark those terms estimated, test their ordering against observed traces, and block operational promotion if the model cannot distinguish meaningful choices reliably. Hero skills are exogenous to this prediction: tap each visibly ready skill, make any required ordinary target selection from the current valid visible targets using the combat target ranking, wait for its resolved report/frame, and replan. Do not add a second skill-specific effect model here.
4. **One authorized Campaign loop.** Compose the existing qualified Campaign stage → Hero Formation route with a new explicit battle-start operation through the canonical core runner and a Campaign battle mutation adapter. The adapter reuses shared castle verification, authorization, and journaling primitives without inserting Campaign into `DailyQuestId` or extending diamond budgets to mean AP. Immediately before entry, reobserve the stage/castle/cost, require the matching authorization, and durably reserve the observed AP cost and sole attempt before tapping Challenge. Reconcile an accepted/rejected start against subsequent observations; leave ambiguous starts consumed and stopped, including across process restarts. During battle, act only when the board and turn are stable and control belongs to the player; rebind coordinates to the newest frame, execute one observed action via the existing tap/swipe executor, and wait for a new settled frame or terminal result. Bound turn count, waiting, and repeated identical states. Replace the runner's single required exit-screen contract with one typed exit policy: routed workflows navigate to their declared screen, while this battle workflow verifies its current observed terminal state without navigation. Migrate existing workflow specs to the routed policy, and return a typed victory, defeat, or stopped/unknown result with the last frame and artifacts; a defeat or unknown stop must not be rewritten as a navigation failure or trigger a restart. Retain the prep-only Campaign task for its distinct navigation contract, not as a competing battle loop.
5. **Canonical documentation.** Add a match-battle workflow note under `docs/game-reference/workflows/` with the three special semantics, click-versus-swap behavior, evidence tiers, client build/provenance, combat-model assumptions, live authority, and remaining uncertainty. Update only the active Campaign/vision design documents whose contracts actually change. Document every added public function and non-obvious helper, with meaningful typed interfaces and fail-fast errors for genuinely invalid content; avoid validation rules that prohibit valid board layouts or future rule implementations. Keep any extension seam at the rule/policy boundary justified by these three distinct effects, not a generic plugin framework.

## Verification and gates

- **Rules:** deterministic tests for legal/illegal swaps, ordinary three, straight four/five, T/L five, 2×2 square, overlap precedence, color-clearing versus color-independent 3×3/cross, edge clipping, special clicks, and stable tie-breaking. Test pure combat-state transitions/target ranking against authored traces, including enemy defeat, player defeat, blocked turn, and unknown evidence. Do not assert a particular server refill or special spawn coordinate without a confirming trace.
- **Vision and loop:** fixture-based tests for board/special/HP/readiness recognition, publisher parity, stale geometry rejection, one action per fresh settled frame, hero skill resolution, terminal detection, and no action on occlusion/unknown. Exercise the typed exit policy with victory, defeat, and stopped/unknown results, plus existing routed workflows; none may hide a battle result behind exit navigation. Test AP authorization against the observed cost, durable reservation before input, rejected/accepted reconciliation, and no replay after an ambiguous start or process restart. Keep observations and action traces under `.local-data/`; promote only reviewed non-secret fixtures to `tests/data/`.
- **Offline commands:** run the focused new component group with `py tools/run_tests.py group <qualified-group>`, then `py tools/run_tests.py affected --base origin/main --explain`; use `py tools/run_tests.py full` for the shared observation/mutation-contract integration. Check documentation diffs with `git diff --check`.
- **Live boundary:** use the repository’s canonical live lease and configured `testing` target. Before the only battle-start action, observe active castle, selected unlocked stage, its AP cost (≤20), readiness, and sufficient AP; record the pre-entry frame and durably reserve exactly that cost and one attempt before input. Within that one attempt, first verify the board classifier and selected action against the live settled frame, then allow the loop to reach a clearly recognized victory or defeat, recording frame/action/HP/turn/skill/result and authorization evidence. A completed battle is a loop proof, not a win-rate benchmark. If the current build differs, recognition/model confidence is inadequate, any action result is ambiguous, or the attempt is exhausted before acceptance, stop; do not spend or replay to complete the plan without fresh authorization. No live action is performed while writing this plan.

## Dependencies and limits

V13/V14 Campaign navigation/formation qualification is a prerequisite for safe battle entry; do not bypass it with raw coordinates. The 20-AP/one-attempt ceiling may provide a first board capture but not enough evidence to calibrate every damage rule and prove a complete loop. In that case, keep the pure solver and fixture results, report the exact live blocker, and request a separately scoped observation or attempt later. Arena/PvP turns, individual hero-effect forecasting, formation optimization, direct socket automation, arbitrary battle modes, and guaranteed wins are out of scope. The shared board engine should be reusable by a later Arena workflow without adding Arena-specific branches now.
